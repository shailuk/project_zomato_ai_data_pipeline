Step 6 : Log into your snowflake account.

STORAGE_INTEGRATION - A Storage Integration is a special security agreement between Snowflake and your cloud provider (AWS S3).
STAGE - A Stage is a pointer location where raw data files sit right before they get unpacked and loaded into structured Snowflake tables.

Step 9 : Create a policy - Add S3 read and list access. aws/iam/zomato_s3_read.json

Step 10 : Create a new role and attach the policy created in the previous step. 

Step 11 : Make sure to add the STORAGE_AWS_EXTERNAL_ID and STORAGE_AWS_IAM_USER_ARN to the Trusted Policy of the role created in the previous step. 

Step 12 : Create File Format - 03_stage_and_formats.sql

            Anatomy of copy command - 
            COPY INTO <target table>
            FROM @aws_stage
            file_format = <file_format>
            files = <file_names>

In short: dbt acts like a smart factory manager for your data.
Instead of writing long, messy SQL scripts to clean your data by hand, dbt lets you write small, organized SQL recipes.
 It then runs those recipes inside your database (like Snowflake) to turn messy raw data into clean tables ready for reports.
